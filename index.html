clipfarm

Clips you approve, from video you own.

Self-hosted software that turns long-form video into short vertical clips with burned-in captions — then posts the ones you approve to your own accounts.

How it works

  • Point it at footage you own or are licensed to cut. Provenance is recorded per file.
  • It transcribes locally, finds self-contained moments, reframes to 9:16 tracking the speaker, and burns word-synced captions.
  • Every clip runs nine compliance checks — duration, aspect, watermark, loudness, sharpness and duplicate detection — before you ever see it.
  • You approve each clip by hand. Nothing publishes on its own.

Where your data lives

On your machine. There is no clipfarm server and no clipfarm account: video, transcripts, tokens and metadata stay in local files and a local database. Access tokens sit in a .env file with permissions 0600 and are used only to call the platform's API from your own computer.

TikTok integration

clipfarm uses Login Kit so you can authorise your own account, and the Content Posting API to send an approved clip to your TikTok drafts — or, once approved for direct posting, straight to your profile. It requests no scopes for reading your content, followers, messages or analytics.
