clipfarm — Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

The short version

clipfarm runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics. We — the authors — receive nothing: not your video, not your captions, not your tokens, not usage data. There is no backend to send it to.

TikTok data

With your authorisation, clipfarm requests user.info.basic to confirm which account you are posting to, and video.upload / video.publish to send clips you have approved. The access token is stored locally and used only to call TikTok's API from your machine. We never see it. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings, and delete the token by removing it from your .env file.

clipfarm does not read your TikTok content, followers, messages or analytics, and does not request scopes for any of those.

Optional AI review

If — and only if — you supply an API key for a third-party model provider, clipfarm sends that provider the clip's transcript and a small number of sampled still frames so it can score the clip before you publish it. This is off by default. The provider's own privacy policy governs that data. Remove the key and the feature stops.

Children

clipfarm is a production tool for content creators and is not directed at children.

Your control

Because everything is local, deleting your data means deleting the files: the storage/ folder and the .env file. Nothing survives elsewhere.

This document describes software that runs entirely on the operator's own machine. It is not legal advice.
