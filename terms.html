clipfarm — Terms of Service

Last updated 19 September 2026

1. What clipfarm is

clipfarm is self-hosted software that runs on your own computer. It turns long-form video you own or are licensed to use into short vertical clips, and — at your explicit instruction — uploads those clips to social accounts you control.

There is no clipfarm service, server or account. The software runs locally. We do not operate infrastructure that receives your video, your captions or your credentials.

2. Your responsibilities

  • You may only process footage you own or are licensed to create derivative works from. Recording provenance is your responsibility; the software provides a field for it but cannot verify it.
  • You may only connect social accounts you own or are authorised to operate.
  • You are responsible for complying with the terms of every platform you post to, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and with the rules of any campaign or brand you produce clips for.
  • You must not use clipfarm to post content you lack rights to, to operate accounts deceptively, or to inflate or manipulate engagement metrics.

3. Posting is never automatic

Clips do not reach any platform without an explicit approval action by you inside the application. Automated quality and compliance checks may block a clip, but they can never publish one.

4. Third-party platforms and services

When you connect an account, you authorise it through that platform's own login page. clipfarm never receives or stores your platform password. Your use of each platform remains governed by that platform's terms.

If you choose to configure optional AI review features, clip transcripts and sampled frames are sent to the provider whose API key you supply, under that provider's terms. These features are off unless you add a key.
